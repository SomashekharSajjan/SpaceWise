SpaceWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

SpaceWise is a storage-management application that helps users understand and manage their Google Gmail and Google Drive data.

Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, SpaceWise receives basic account information such as your Google account ID, name, and email address.

If you choose to connect Google services, SpaceWise may access information from Gmail and Google Drive according to the permissions you authorize.

How We Use Information

We use the information only to provide and operate SpaceWise features, including Gmail cleanup, Google Drive analysis, duplicate detection, and related storage-management functionality.

Google User Data

SpaceWise uses Google user data only for the functionality described above and does not sell Google user data to third parties.

Data Storage

Account and service-connection information may be stored on our application servers to provide authenticated access to SpaceWise.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate SpaceWise.

Deleting Your Data

You may stop using SpaceWise and request deletion of your account information by contacting the SpaceWise support email associated with this application.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or SpaceWise, please contact the application support email provided on the SpaceWise Google authorization page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when SpaceWise changes. Any updated version will be published on this page.
